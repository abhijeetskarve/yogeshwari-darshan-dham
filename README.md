# Yogeshwari Darshan Dham

Create a beautiful, easy-to-navigate digital platform/website for Yogeshwari Devi Ambajogai situated in Beed Maharashtra India. The website could include temple history, darshan and aarti information, festival updates, travel guidance, approved photographs and videos, puja enquiries, and other useful services for devotees sections. 



Idea is, this digital platform/website should prioritize five experiences:

Feel the temple: a beautiful homepage with authentic temple photography, devotional elements and a warm welcome.



Connect with Mata: approved darshan photographs, aarti recordings and festival updates.



Plan a pilgrimage: clear directions to Ambajogai, transport information and practical travel guidance for one to travel through air, by road or by train.



Stay connected: important dates, announcements, puja enquiries and temple committee information.



Preserve our heritage: the history of Yogeshwari Mata and the traditions of our Konkani community.
